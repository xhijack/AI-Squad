# Brand voice and fact check

## Voice
1. Read `docs/BRAND.md`. Compare the draft sentence by sentence with the voice description and examples.
2. Replace words from the "avoid" list; prefer the "use" list.
3. Check reading level, length and format rules for the channel.
4. Visuals: colours, logo use, fonts and imagery follow the visual rules.

## Claims
1. List every factual claim (numbers, comparisons, promises, quotes, awards).
2. Each claim needs a source in the brief or a cited public source; otherwise rewrite it or mark it for the owner.
3. No invented testimonials, numbers or quotes. Nothing that the offer does not include.

## Result
Write the result in the handoff: "voice OK / fixed: …", "claims: 5 checked, 1 removed (no source)".
