# Content calendar

## Each row in the calendar
Date · channel · piece (title or topic) · audience · key message · format · KPI · owner · status (TODO / DRAFT / REVIEW / APPROVED / PUBLISHED).

## Planning steps
1. Start from the brief's goals and KPIs; every piece serves one of them.
2. Spread themes over the period: education, proof, offer, community. Reuse one core piece across channels (article → posts → email).
3. Respect each channel's rhythm and the team's real capacity; fewer good pieces beat many weak ones.
4. Put fixed dates first (launches, holidays, events), then fill in.
5. Mirror the work items on the board (`docs/BOARD.md`) so owners and status are visible.

## Working from the calendar
Pick only pieces assigned to you; drafts go to `content/drafts/` or `content/social/<channel>/`; hand off for the brand/voice review.
