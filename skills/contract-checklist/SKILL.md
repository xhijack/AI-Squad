# Contract checklist

A drafting aid, not legal advice: anything marked **lawyer** goes to the owner with the recommendation to ask a
licensed advocate. Write the result at the end of the contract as `## Legal checklist`, one line per item:
`- [x] item: how it is met (clause)`, `- [ ] item: what is missing`, or `- [-] item: not applicable, why`.

## 1. Valid agreement (Pasal 1320 KUHPerdata)
1. **Agreement (sepakat):** offer and acceptance are clear; no clause that one party could say it never agreed to.
2. **Capacity and authority (kecakapan):** each signatory may bind its party: for a PT, the director(s) named in the
   articles of association (Anggaran Dasar) or a written power of attorney; check whether board approval is needed.
3. **A specific subject (hal tertentu):** scope, deliverables, quantities and dates are defined well enough to tell
   when they are met (an annex for the scope).
4. **A lawful cause (sebab yang halal):** nothing against the law, public order or morality.
Missing 1 or 2 makes the contract voidable; missing 3 or 4 makes it void: never leave these open.

## 2. Language
- An agreement with an Indonesian party is in Bahasa Indonesia (UU 24/2009 Pasal 31, Perpres 63/2019); a bilingual
  text is fine. State which text prevails (normally Indonesian).

## 3. Personal data (UU 27/2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi)
Only when the work involves personal data (an ERP with customer, employee or patient data usually does):
- who is the controller and who the processor; the purpose and the lawful basis;
- security measures, confidentiality of staff, sub-processors only with consent;
- notice of a personal data breach within the time the law sets (3 x 24 hours to data subjects and the authority),
  and cooperation in the response;
- transfers outside Indonesia only under the law's conditions; return or deletion at the end; data subject requests.
Mark **lawyer** for health, financial or children's data.

## 4. Signature and stamp duty
- **Electronic signature:** valid under UU ITE (Pasal 11, as amended by UU 1/2024) and PP 71/2019 when the signing
  data is linked to and controlled by the signer, changes are detectable and the signer is identified. Prefer a
  certified electronic signature (a PSrE registered in Indonesia) for high value or contested contracts.
- **Stamp duty (bea meterai, UU 10/2020):** state who pays; e-meterai for electronic documents.
- Signature blocks: name, title and authority basis of each signatory; placeholders only in the repository.

## 5. Commercial points (compare with `docs/LEGAL-POLICY.md`)
Scope and acceptance (UAT), price and payment terms and taxes (PPN, PPh 23 for services), change requests,
intellectual property and licences, confidentiality and its period, warranty and support, limitation of liability
(a cap, no unlimited liability), penalties, term and termination, force majeure, governing law and dispute
resolution (court or arbitration such as BANI), notices, assignment.
Every deviation from the policy is listed for the owner.

## Never
- Never set `STATUS: APPROVED`, never send or sign a contract, never put parties' personal data in the repository.
