# Marketing brief intake

The source documents are `docs/MARKETING-BRIEF.md`, `docs/BRAND.md` and `docs/CHANNELS.md` (status line at the top).

## Interview the owner (a few questions at a time)
- **Brief:** business goal for the next 3 months, product or offer, price, audience (who, where, what they care about), competitors, KPIs with targets (traffic, leads, sales, followers), budget, deadline.
- **Brand:** personality in three words, words to use and to avoid, tone examples they like and dislike, logo, colours, fonts, imagery rules.
- **Channels:** which channels exist (website, blog, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, email, ads), accounts and who owns them, posting capacity, important dates.

## Then
1. Draft each document in its skeleton; mark guesses with `ASSUMPTION:`.
2. Every KPI gets a target, a measurement method and a reporting rhythm.
3. Show the owner a summary; set `STATUS: APPROVED` only after their approval.
4. No content, posts or campaigns start before all three are approved.
