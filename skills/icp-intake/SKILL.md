# ICP, offer and pipeline intake

The source documents are `docs/ICP.md`, `docs/OFFER.md` and `docs/PIPELINE.md` (status line at the top).

## Interview the owner (a few questions at a time)
- **ICP:** best current customers and why, segments (industry, size, region), the buyer's role, pains, buying signals, who is not a fit.
- **Offer:** products and packages, prices and who may give discounts, proof (cases, results), common objections and the answers.
- **Pipeline:** the stages a deal goes through, the exit criterion of each stage, the CRM in use and its required fields, consent rules for contacting people.

## Then
1. Draft each document in its skeleton; mark guesses with `ASSUMPTION:`.
2. Show the owner a summary; set `STATUS: APPROVED` only after their approval.
3. No research batch, sequence or CRM change starts before all three are approved.
4. Never write real contact data into these documents.
