# Accessibility check

Check every screen of the module; report failures like design findings.

1. **Contrast:** body text at least 4.5:1, large text and icons at least 3:1 against their background.
2. **Keyboard:** every action works with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space; the order follows the layout; no keyboard traps.
3. **Focus:** a clearly visible focus indicator on every interactive element.
4. **Names and labels:** form fields have labels; icon buttons have accessible names; headings form a sensible outline.
5. **Images:** meaningful images have alt text; decorative ones have empty alt.
6. **Touch targets:** at least 44 × 44 px on mobile, with spacing.
7. **Zoom and reflow:** usable at 200 % zoom and at 320 px width without horizontal scrolling.
8. **Motion and colour:** information is never by colour alone; animations respect reduced motion.
9. **Errors:** messages say what went wrong and how to fix it, next to the field.
