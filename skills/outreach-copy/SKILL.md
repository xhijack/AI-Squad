# Outreach copy

## A sequence (in `sales/drafts/<segment>-<name>.md`)
- **Goal** (a reply, a call, a demo) and **segment** from `docs/ICP.md`.
- **Steps:** channel, day, subject line, body, call to action, and the objection the step handles (from `docs/OFFER.md`).
- Usually 3 to 5 steps over 2 to 3 weeks; every step adds something new (insight, proof, question), never "just following up".

## Writing rules
- Open with the prospect's situation (a signal or pain from the ICP), not with us.
- One clear call to action; short paragraphs; plain language; no false urgency.
- Personalisation as placeholders: `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{signal}}`. Never real contact data in drafts.
- Prices and promises only as `docs/OFFER.md` allows.
- Honest subject, clear sender, an easy opt-out line.

Every draft stays a draft until the owner approves it; you never send anything.
