# Market research

## Before you start
- Write the question in one sentence and what decision it serves.
- Read the Company Brain's `research/` and `docs/RESEARCH.md`: start from what is known and when it was found.

## While researching
- Prefer primary sources (the company's own data, official statistics, the competitor's own pages and prices, real
  reviews) over articles about them. Note the date of every source; anything older than a year is marked as such.
- Competitors: what they offer, for whom, at what price (public), how they sell, what their customers praise and
  complain about. One row per competitor in a table.
- Users: the job they try to get done, what they use now, what it costs them; quotes only from public reviews or the
  company's own feedback, never personal data.

## Writing it down
- In `docs/RESEARCH.md`: the question, the findings (each with source and date), what you conclude (marked as your
  conclusion), the confidence (high / medium / low) and what would change it.
- A finding the whole company should keep: propose it as a Company Brain document under `research/<topic>.md`
  (propose-learning, kind brain). Keep that document current rather than adding a new one per question.
- Report to the lead: the answer in three lines, the confidence, and the open questions.
